Course Overview

What This Module Covers

Our RTR(A) module delivers comprehensive training in radio communication procedures, ICAO phraseology, and communication protocols essential for safe aviation operations, as mandated by DGCA and ICAO standards. From routine ATC exchanges to emergency declarations — you will be heard clearly and understood completely.

Curriculum Highlights

What You Will Study

Radio Communication Basics

  • Radio equipment operation
  • Frequency management
  • Signal characteristics and quality
  • Communication protocols

ICAO Phraseology

  • Standard phraseology procedures
  • Phonetic alphabet and numbers
  • Time and position reporting
  • Navigation and approach calls

Air Traffic Control

  • ATC communication procedures
  • Clearance acknowledgment
  • Traffic information exchange
  • Approach and departure communications

Emergency Communications

  • Distress and urgency signals
  • Emergency phraseology
  • Search and rescue communications
  • Pan-pan and Mayday procedures

Weather Communications

  • Weather information requests
  • Pilot weather reports (PIREP)
  • ATIS and weather briefings
  • Weather deviation requests

International Operations

  • International flight communications
  • Oceanic and remote area procedures
  • Language proficiency requirements
  • Cultural communication considerations
