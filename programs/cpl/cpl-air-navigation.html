Course Overview

What This Module Covers

Our Navigation & Flight Planning module equips aspiring commercial pilots with in-depth knowledge of navigation principles, advanced techniques, and contemporary aviation systems aligned with DGCA standards. You will develop the ability to plan and execute flights accurately across diverse conditions.

Curriculum Highlights

What You Will Study

Foundational Navigation Concepts

  • Earth coordinate system and mapping
  • Great circles and rhumb line theory
  • Direction and distance computation
  • Charting fundamentals and map reading

Navigation Instruments and Aids

  • Magnetic compass systems
  • Gyroscopic instrument operation
  • Radio navigation aids (VOR, DME, ILS)
  • GPS and GNSS technology

Route and Flight Planning

  • Route optimisation and selection
  • Weather impact assessment
  • Fuel calculation and management
  • Alternate routing strategy
