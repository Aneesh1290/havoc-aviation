Course Overview

What This Module Covers

Our Air Law & Regulations module delivers in-depth training on Indian aviation legislation, DGCA directives, international aviation standards, and the complete legal framework that underpins commercial aviation operations. Designed to build both exam readiness and real-world regulatory awareness.

Curriculum Highlights

What You Will Study

Indian Aviation Law

  • Aircraft Act, Rules and Amendments
  • DGCA regulations and circulars
  • Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR)
  • National aviation policy framework

International Standards

  • ICAO annexes and standards
  • Chicago Convention principles
  • International flight operations
  • Bilateral aviation agreements

Pilot Licensing

  • Licence categories and privileges
  • Medical certification requirements
  • Training and competency standards
  • Licence renewal procedures

Aircraft Operations

  • Airworthiness requirements
  • Aircraft registration and marking
  • Maintenance regulations
  • Flight operations standards

Air Traffic Management

  • Airspace classification and usage
  • ATC procedures and protocols
  • Communication requirements
  • Navigation services regulations

Safety and Security

  • Aviation safety management systems
  • Incident and accident reporting
  • Security regulations and procedures
  • Emergency response protocols
