Course Overview

What This Module Covers

Our Aircraft Technical (General) module delivers comprehensive training in core aircraft systems, aerodynamics principles, and engineering concepts essential for commercial pilot operations. The curriculum is fully aligned with DGCA standards, combining detailed theory with practical technical applications.

Curriculum Highlights

What You Will Study

Aerodynamics

  • Principles of flight
  • Lift, drag, thrust and weight
  • Airflow patterns and behaviour
  • Stall and spin characteristics

Aircraft Structures

  • Airframe construction methods
  • Materials and stress analysis
  • Control surfaces and mechanisms
  • Landing gear systems

Powerplant Systems

  • Engine types and operation
  • Fuel and lubrication systems
  • Propeller systems and operation
  • Engine performance and limitations

Aircraft Systems

  • Electrical systems and components
  • Hydraulic and pneumatic systems
  • Environmental control systems
  • Fire protection and detection

Flight Instruments

  • Primary flight instruments
  • Engine monitoring systems
  • Navigation instruments
  • Electronic flight displays

Performance and Limitations

  • Weight and balance calculations
  • Performance charts and graphs
  • Takeoff and landing performance
  • Aircraft limitations and placards
