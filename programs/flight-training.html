Flight Training

Train with modern aircraft and experienced instructors at our partner flying schools, ensuring your transition from ground to sky is seamless and safe.

Course Description

Our flight training program is conducted in partnership with DGCA-approved flying schools equipped with modern aircraft and state-of-the-art facilities. From your first solo flight to commercial pilot license, we guide you through every milestone.

With experienced flight instructors who are committed to safety and excellence, you'll develop the skills and confidence needed to become a professional pilot.

Training Phases:

  • Private Pilot License (PPL) - 40 hours minimum
  • Commercial Pilot License (CPL) - 200 hours minimum
  • Instrument Rating (IR) training
  • Multi-Engine Rating (ME) training
  • Night flying and cross-country navigation
  • Radio telephony procedures

Training Schedule
