Course Overview

Line Training Readiness

Our Post Type Rating Preparation course is designed for pilots who have completed their type rating and are preparing for airline line training or transitioning to operational flying. This program focuses on real-world operational scenarios and airline-specific procedures.

How We Train

Training Methods

Simulator Training

High-fidelity simulator sessions with realistic operational scenarios including weather challenges and system failures.

Case Study Analysis

Real-world incident analysis and discussion of operational challenges faced by airline pilots in daily operations.

Interactive Workshops

Group discussions, role-playing exercises, and peer learning sessions to enhance communication and teamwork skills.
