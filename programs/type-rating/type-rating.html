Type Rating
(A320 / B737 / ATR / Others)

Master your aircraft type through simulator-driven, airline-ready training designed to prepare you for real-world operations from day one.

Course Description

In partnership with DGCA-certified, world-class TRTOs, our program goes beyond certification — it builds true capability. You'll gain in-depth systems knowledge, SOP proficiency, and hands-on cockpit experience aligned with global airline standards.

You're not just earning a type rating — you're becoming airline-capable and cockpit-ready with the confidence to handle real-world scenarios from your first day of line training.

Available Aircraft Types:

  • Airbus A320 Family (A319/A320/A321)
  • Boeing B737 (B737-800/900/MAX)
  • ATR 42/72 Regional Aircraft
  • Embraer E-Jets (E170/E175/E190/E195)
  • Bombardier Q400

Training Components:

  • Ground school (systems, procedures, performance)
  • CBT (Computer-Based Training) modules
  • Full flight simulator training (40+ hours)
  • Line-oriented flight training (LOFT)
  • DGCA skill test and license validation

Training Schedule

Duration: 6-8 weeks
Simulator Hours: 40+ hours
Ground School: 120 hours
TRTO Partner: Partnered Training Centers
