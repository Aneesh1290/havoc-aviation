Simulator Assessment Preparation

Master flight simulator evaluations for airline selection success

Course Overview

Our Simulator Assessment Preparation course is designed to help pilots excel in flight simulator evaluations during airline selection processes. This comprehensive program combines theoretical knowledge with extensive hands-on simulator training to ensure candidates demonstrate their flying skills, decision-making abilities, and crew resource management competencies effectively.

Assessment Areas Covered

Flight Performance

  • Standard operating procedures (SOPs)
  • Aircraft control and handling
  • Flight path management
  • Normal and abnormal procedures
  • Approach and landing techniques

Technical Skills

  • Aircraft systems knowledge
  • Instrument flying proficiency
  • Weather interpretation
  • Navigation accuracy
  • Performance calculations and awareness

Crew Resource Management

  • Communication skills
  • Leadership and followership
  • Situational awareness
  • Workload management
  • Decision-making processes

Behavioral Assessment

  • Time management
  • Adaptability to change
  • Professional behavior
  • Learning agility
  • Stress management
