Airline Selection Preparation

Crack the toughest airline selection processes with confidence. From written exams and simulator assessments to HR rounds and psychometric evaluations.

Course Description

At Havoc Aviation, we have a strong foundation in airline exam and interview preparation — it's where our journey began. Trusted by aspiring pilots and professionals across India, our track record speaks for itself.

With deep industry experience and a results-driven approach, we've helped hundreds secure their dream airline job with domestic and international airlines.

Our Comprehensive Training Includes:

  • Written Test Prep: Master technical and aptitude-based questions with precision
  • Group Exercises & Discussions: Build communication, leadership, and decision-making skills
  • Psychometric & Aptitude Training: With our in-house aviation psychologist and digital evaluation setup
  • Mock HR & Technical Interviews: Practice with real airline captains and assessors
  • Simulator Assessment Prep: Hands-on training for airline simulation tests
  • Airline-Specific Preparation: Customized training for different airline requirements

  • SpiceJet
