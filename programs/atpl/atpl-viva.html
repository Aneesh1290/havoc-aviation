Course Overview

What This Module Covers

Our ATPL Viva preparation course provides intensive training for the oral examination component of the ATPL certification, covering all subjects and practical scenarios faced by airline transport pilots.

ATPL Exam Focused
Experienced DGCA Examiners
Mock Oral Examinations

Viva Preparation Areas

Key Subject Coverage

General Navigation

  • Advanced navigation principles
  • Great circle and rhumb line calculations
  • Flight planning and fuel calculations
  • Navigation system failures

Radio Aids and Instruments

  • ILS and precision approach systems
  • GPS and RNAV procedures
  • Communication system operations
  • Instrument failure scenarios

Air Meteorology

  • Weather system analysis
  • Hazardous weather recognition
  • Weather routing decisions
  • Meteorology and chart interpretation

Air Regulations

  • DGCA regulations and circulars
  • International aviation law
  • Operational procedures
  • Regulatory compliance scenarios

Aircraft Systems

  • Advanced aircraft systems
  • Emergency procedures
  • System failures and troubleshooting
  • Performance calculations

Flight Operations

  • Crew resource management
  • Decision-making processes
  • Risk assessment and mitigation
  • Operational scenario discussions
